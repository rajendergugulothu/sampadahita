From Corporate Technology to Financial Advocate

Nithya V. Ananda · Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Nithya spent more than two decades in enterprise technology and financial services — leading release and configuration management for organizations including RGA Reinsurance, Wells Fargo Advisors, and the global bank ANZ. An engineer by training (B.E., Electrical & Electronics, NIT Kurukshetra), Nithya spent those years bringing order and clarity to complex systems.

Working alongside high-earning professionals, Nithya noticed a troubling pattern: brilliant people, earning well, were quietly overpaying taxes and under-planning for retirement — not from carelessness, but because no one had ever explained the strategy in plain language.

That realization became SampadaHita — “well-wisher of your wealth.” The mission is simple: demystify personal finance, and give families bespoke strategies to reduce risk, lower taxes, and work toward a tax-free retirement. Education first. Sales pressure never.
