Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers, no jargon.

Do you charge a fee for the initial consultation?
No. Your initial financial analysis and strategy conversation are completely free and carry no obligation. We believe you should understand your options before deciding anything.
What makes SampadaHita different?
We lead with education, not sales. Our name means “well-wisher of your wealth” — we explain the “why” behind every strategy in plain language, and the decision is always yours.
Do I have to move all my investments to you?
Not at all. We meet you where you are. Many clients start with a single goal — like tax strategy or retirement income — and expand from there only if and when it makes sense.
What's the difference between tax preparation and tax planning?
Tax preparation looks backward — filing what already happened. Tax planning looks forward — structuring your income, investments, and business proactively so you legally owe less over your lifetime.
How often will we review my strategy?
We schedule periodic reviews — typically at least annually and whenever your life changes — to make sure your plan keeps pace with your goals, your family, and the tax code.
Can you help me become a finance professional?
Yes. Through our education and training programs, we help people learn to manage their own family's wealth or build a career as a financial strategist. See our Partnership page.
